Fish Tank To-Do

A to-do app where every task is a fish. Finish something and watch it join the tank.

  • If-then tasks wait in a bubble until their condition is met.
  • Guilty pleasures are treats priced in pearls you earn by finishing tasks.
  • Local-first — works with no account and no network. Sign in with Google only if you want sync across devices.
